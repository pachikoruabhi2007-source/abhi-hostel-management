Terms and Conditions

Please read these terms and conditions carefully before booking or residing at Executive PG & Private Hostel.

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Agreement to Terms

By accessing our online portal, registering an account, booking a room/bed, or making payments, you agree to be bound by these Terms and Conditions. These terms apply to all residents, students, working professionals, and guests staying at Executive PG & Private Hostel.

2. Resident Eligibility & Identity Verification

  • All prospective residents must provide valid government-issued photo identification (such as Aadhaar Card, Passport, Voter ID, or Driving License) prior to or upon physical check-in.
  • Working professionals must submit employee ID / employment proof; students must submit valid college ID / coaching admission receipts.
  • Providing fraudulent or forged identity proofs will result in immediate disqualification and reporting to law enforcement authorities.

3. Rent and Payment Terms

  • Monthly Rent: Monthly stay fees are determined based on the selected sharing type (Single, Double, Triple sharing) and room tier (AC / Non-AC).
  • Due Date: Monthly stay fees must be settled between the 1st and 5th of each calendar month. Payments can be completed online via UPI, Net Banking, Debit/Credit Cards using our Razorpay payment portal.
  • Security Deposit: A standard refundable security deposit is payable upon admission. This deposit acts as security against property damage, unpaid stay dues, and notice period adherence.
  • Receipts: An authentic digital receipt is generated instantly upon successful transaction completion and available in the resident portal.

4. Code of Conduct & Hostel Rules

  • Gate Timings: The main hostel entrance gates close strictly at 10:30 PM daily. Late entry without prior warden written permission is prohibited.
  • Prohibited Substances: Smoking, consumption of alcohol, narcotics, gambling, or hazardous materials are strictly prohibited within the hostel premises. Violation will lead to immediate expulsion with forfeiture of deposit.
  • Visitor Policy: Outside visitors are permitted only in designated common lounge areas between 9:00 AM and 7:00 PM. No overnight outside guests are allowed in resident rooms without warden clearance.
  • Quiet Hours: Residents must respect fellow roommates and maintain silence between 10:30 PM and 6:00 AM.

5. Notice Period and Vacating Rules

  • Residents intending to vacate or transfer must submit a formal 15-day notice through the resident portal.
  • Failure to provide the 15-day advance notice may result in deduction of the corresponding days' rent from the security deposit.
  • Full security deposit refund is disbursed within 5–7 working days post room key handover and asset clearance inspection.

6. Limitation of Liability

While the hostel maintains 24/7 CCTV surveillance across common corridors and gates, management is not responsible for loss, theft, or damage to personal valuables, laptops, jewelry, or cash left unattended in rooms. Residents are advised to keep their personal lockers secured.
