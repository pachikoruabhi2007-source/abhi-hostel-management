Cancellation and Refund Policy

Clear, transparent guidelines regarding security deposit refunds, room booking cancellations, and fee adjustments.

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Policy Overview

At Executive PG & Private Hostel, we strive to maintain complete transparency in our billing, accommodation fees, and security deposit management. This Cancellation and Refund Policy outlines the terms governing cancellations, refunds of security deposits, and payment dispute resolutions.

2. Security Deposit Refund Policy

✓ Security Deposit Refund Timeline: 5 to 7 Working Days

Security deposits are 100% refundable upon completing room clearance and key handover, provided the mandatory 15-day notice was served.

  • Eligibility: The resident must submit a formal vacating notice via the resident portal at least 15 days prior to their intended check-out date.
  • Clearance Inspection: On the check-out date, the resident will surrender their room keys and a joint room inspection will be conducted to verify that hostel assets (bed, cupboard, AC/geyser fixtures) are undamaged.
  • Deductions: Deductions from the security deposit may occur only for:
    • Unpaid monthly stay rent or utility dues.
    • Physical damages to hostel property beyond normal wear and tear.
    • Shortfall in the mandatory 15-day vacating notice period.
  • Disbursement: The net refundable deposit will be credited directly to the resident's bank account or original payment method within 5–7 business days post check-out.

3. Advance Room Booking & Admission Cancellation

  • Cancellation 48+ Hours Prior to Move-in Date: If a prospective resident books a room/bed in advance and cancels at least 48 hours before their scheduled check-in date, a 100% full refund of any paid security deposit will be processed.
  • Cancellation within 48 Hours of Move-in Date: A nominal administrative processing fee of ₹500 may be deducted, and the balance deposit will be refunded.
  • No-Show without Notice: If an applicant fails to check in on the reserved entry date without prior communication, the reservation will be held for 24 hours, after which the room will be released.

4. Monthly Stay Fee / Rent Policy

  • Monthly stay rent is paid for the ongoing calendar month and is non-refundable once the billing month commences and the room is occupied.
  • Residents who vacate mid-month without serving the mandatory advance notice are not entitled to a pro-rata refund of the ongoing month's rent.

5. Duplicate or Accidental Payments

  • In the event a resident inadvertently makes a duplicate payment for the same billing cycle (e.g. multiple UPI debits due to bank network delays), the excess amount is 100% refunded.
  • To report a duplicate transaction, email your transaction ID and bank debit screenshot to pachikoruabhi2007@gmail.com.
  • The excess amount will be verified and refunded to the source bank account/UPI ID within 3 to 5 business days.

6. Mode of Refund Settlement

All approved refunds will be credited back via the original payment method used during the transaction (via Razorpay / UPI) or directly to the verified bank account provided in writing by the resident.
