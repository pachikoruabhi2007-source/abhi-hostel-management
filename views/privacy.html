Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. Learn how Executive PG & Private Hostel collects, protects, and handles your information.

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Overview & Commitment

This Privacy Policy explains how Executive PG & Private Hostel ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and protects personal data obtained from residents, prospective applicants, and visitors navigating our portal. We are committed to safeguarding user data in compliance with the Information Technology Act, 2000 and applicable data protection regulations in India.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to provide accommodation, ensure security, and process hostel stay fees:

  • Personal Identity Information: Full name, email address, mobile phone number, emergency contact details, permanent native address.
  • Verification Documents: Government photo identification proof (e.g. Aadhaar Card, Passport, Voter ID) required for local police verification and tenant safety registers.
  • Stay & Billing Records: Assigned room number, check-in date, payment history, transaction references, food preference.
  • Technical & Session Data: IP address, device browser type, and secure authentication session cookies used solely to keep your account signed in.
🛡️
Payment Card Data Protection (PCI-DSS):
We NEVER store your debit/credit card numbers, CVVs, or Net Banking passwords on our servers. All financial transactions are encrypted and processed through RBI-authorized payment aggregators (Razorpay / UPI).

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly for legitimate hostel administrative functions:

  • Processing bed reservations, room assignments, and resident onboarding.
  • Generating verifiable digital rent receipts and fee collection records.
  • Resolving maintenance requests, food service feedback, and vacating notices.
  • Communicating critical announcements (e.g., gate closures, emergency alerts, fee due notifications).
  • Complying with statutory local municipal and law enforcement resident record requirements.

4. Information Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party advertisers or telemarketers under any circumstances. Information is disclosed solely in the following limited situations:

  • Authorized Payment Gateways: To Razorpay / UPI payment networks solely to verify and complete transaction settlements.
  • Legal & Law Enforcement Authorities: When formally requested by Indian police or municipal authorities pursuant to legal subpoenas or statutory guest verification laws.

5. Data Security Measures

We employ industry-standard security safeguards to protect your personal data from unauthorized access or theft:

  • Encrypted Passwords: Resident passwords are cryptographically hashed using Bcrypt before storage. Plain-text passwords are never saved.
  • ID Document Masking: Sensitive government identification numbers (Aadhaar numbers) are masked (e.g. •••• •••• 1234) on public portal profiles.
  • HTTPS Encryption: All data transmitted between your browser and our servers is secured with SSL/TLS encryption.
  • Session Hardening: Secure, HTTP-Only cookies prevent cross-site scripting (XSS) and unauthorized token hijacking.

6. Data Retention & Resident Rights

Resident stay data and financial receipts are retained as required by financial accounting and taxation laws. Residents have the right to inspect their stored profile, request corrections, or ask for account archival upon vacating the premises.
